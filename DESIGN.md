## 📐 Layout & Shapes

- **Sidebar Width:** `280px`
- **Base Grid Unit:** `4px`
- **Standard Radius:** `4px` (Buttons, Inputs)
- **Large Radius:** `8px` (Cards, Modals)
- **Pill Radius:** `999px` (Status Badges)

---

## 🏛️ Design Principles

1. **Clarity & Efficiency:** Designed for high-stakes hospitality management.
2. **Institutional Aesthetic:** Professional and reliable, letting data take center stage.
3. **Semantic Hierarchy:** Color is used strictly for status-driven information.
4. **Legibility:** Optimized for data-dense environments using the Inter typeface.
